Project Highlights

A nature-led retreat rooted in its farmland.

Project Type
Nature Retreat / Hospitality Masterplanning
Location
Puranpur, near Pilibhit, Uttar Pradesh
Area
4 Acres · 16,187 sqm
Components
22 Cottages · Amenities & Farmland

Designed as a nature-led river retreat in Puranpur, this 4-acre hospitality development is planned as a low-density, experience-led destination integrating accommodation, recreation, farming and landscape into one cohesive masterplan. The planning responds closely to the existing site context, opening visual axes toward the canal, forest and surrounding farmland while keeping each cottage private, and connected to the land.

The retreat holds 22 units across Machan, independent and interconnected cottages, 2BHK and Bali-style cottages and a private 4BHK cottage. Common facilities — a reception lounge, multipurpose hall, restaurant, games room, yoga deck, pool and landscaped grounds — are organised around a substantial retained farmland field.

A low-impact, experiential masterplan where connecting paths, decks and activity spaces sit with the canal and farmland — a slower, more immersive hospitality experience.

Key Design Approach
Low-density planning with landscape integration, experiential circulation and architecture rooted in the site's agricultural and natural context.
