Located in Kasmoli, Uttarakhand, Agar Hills is a premium hilltop masterplan envisioned as a carefully integrated destination combining residential plots, hospitality, serviced living and shared amenities within a naturally contoured landscape. It is planned around premium plots ranging from 300 to 900 sq.yd., complemented by hotel and suite accommodations, service apartment blocks and a distributed network of amenities.

The masterplan responds directly to the site's steep and varied contours, using the natural topography as the defining element rather than imposing a grid. With a hilltop offering expansive 360-degree views, the planning prioritises view corridors, orientation, privacy and a seamless connection with the Himalayan landscape.

A structured circulation network optimises vehicular and pedestrian movement while balancing developable area with landscaped open space — a terrain-responsive, hospitality-led hill development.

Key Design Approach
Working with the natural topography to create a cohesive development while minimising unnecessary site intervention.
