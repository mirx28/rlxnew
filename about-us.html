The Studio

rl X design studio brings collaboration, craft, and care into every space.

Founded by Rishabh Kalra and Lakshay Jasoria, our studio is built on inclusive, client-first design that balances creativity, functionality, and thoughtful execution from concept through completion.

“

Great design comes from collaborative relationships with our clients.

rl X design studio was founded by Rishabh Kalra and Lakshay Jasoria with a commitment to inclusivity, creativity, and innovation. Our name, which evolved from Rishabh-Lakshay & clients, reflects our belief that great design comes from collaborative relationships with our clients.

We take pride in advocating for the needs and wants of our clients and telling their stories through our work. Our responsibility extends beyond beautiful buildings — it extends to enriching lives, strengthening communities, and contributing to the sustainability of the soul.

We are passionate about creating unique and functional spaces that reflect our clients’ vision and personality, and we deliver exceptional service from concept through value engineering to completion.

Meet the Founders

Co-founders shaping every project with experience and intention.

Rishabh Kalra

Co-founder, Principal Architect

Rishabh trained at the School of Planning and Architecture, Bhopal, and developed his craft with leading practices in India and at Takashi Niwa Architects in Hanoi, Vietnam. He returned to India with a refined eye for site, detail, and the way buildings perform over time.

Lakshay Jasoria

Co-founder, Design Lead

Lakshay also holds a Bachelor of Architecture from SPA Bhopal and brings broad experience across residential, commercial, and hospitality projects. His work is grounded in client stories, practical detailing, and the thoughtful use of materials.
