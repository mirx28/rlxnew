Aambagh
Villa Plots

A plotted villa community in Aambagh, Rishikesh — fourteen parcels, each paired with a villa typology.

Project

A complete villa community, planned and attainable.

Project Type
Residential Plotting + Villa
Location
Aambagh, Rishikesh, Uttarakhand
Plotted Area
1,639 sq.m · 17,647 sq.ft
Components
14 Plots · 2BHK–4BHK Duplex Villas

Conceived as an affordable, fully furnished villa community in Aambagh, Rishikesh, this plotted residential development is planned around a series of carefully proportioned land parcels, each paired with a corresponding villa typology. The project focuses on well-designed, efficient and attainable second places with a strong emphasis on functionality, comfort and a cohesive identity.

The master comprises fourteen plots of roughly 87 to 202 sq.m., allowing a mix of 2BHK, 3BHK and 4BHK duplex villas. The architectural and interior direction is contemporary and nature-responsive — stone, glass, warm textures and natural tones, with large openings and terraces that draw the surrounding environment into the homes.

Key Design Approach
Efficient planning, consistent architectural identity and plot-responsive villa layouts.
